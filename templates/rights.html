⚖️ Voter Rights & Responsibilities

Understanding democratic participation is an important part of being an informed citizen.

🏛️

Right to Vote – Constitutional & Statutory Right

In India, the right to vote is a constitutional and statutory right, rather than a Fundamental Right.

✓

Article 326 of the Constitution provides for elections to the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies on the basis of adult suffrage.

✓

Citizens who meet the legal eligibility requirements and are registered in the electoral roll can vote.

✓

The Representation of the People Acts, 1950 and 1951 provide the statutory framework for elections.

📚

Be Informed

Voters should seek reliable information before making electoral decisions.

✓

Check important election information from official ECI sources.

✓

Learn about candidates and important issues before voting.

✓

Compare information from trustworthy sources.

🔐

Protect Your Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with unknown websites or individuals.

✓

Never share your OTP or password with anyone.

✓

Use only official election websites for voter services.

✓

Be careful of fake calls, messages and websites asking for personal details.

🚫

Reject Misinformation

Verify election-related claims through reliable and official sources.

✓

Check the source and date before trusting information.

✓

Do not forward unverified election news on social media.

✓

Cross-check suspicious claims with official ECI information.

👥

Equal Rights

Every eligible voter has an equal opportunity to participate in the electoral process, without discrimination based on personal characteristics such as caste, religion, gender or language.

🛡️

Free and Fair Elections

The Election Commission of India conducts elections with the aim of ensuring a transparent, impartial and well-managed electoral process.

🪪

Your Responsibility

Vote responsibly, follow election rules, respect democratic values and encourage others to participate in the electoral process.

💡

Did You Know?

  • You can vote only once in a particular election.
  • Your vote is secret and your choice is your own.
  • You have the right to choose candidates based on your own views and beliefs.
  • The Election Commission provides facilities for persons with disabilities and senior citizens.
