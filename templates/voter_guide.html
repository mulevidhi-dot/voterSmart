📝 Voter Guide

Learn the basics of becoming an informed voter.

👤

Who Can Vote?

An Indian citizen who is 18 years of age or older, meets the applicable eligibility requirements, and is registered in the electoral roll can vote in elections.

📝

Voter Registration

New eligible voters can apply for inclusion in the electoral roll using Form 6 through the official Election Commission voter portal.

Eligibility Criteria for Voting

  • Must be an Indian citizen.
  • Must be 18 years of age or older, subject to the applicable qualifying date.
  • The person's name must be included in the electoral roll.
  • The person must satisfy the applicable legal eligibility requirements.

📋

Documents for a New Voter

When applying as a new voter, applicants should check the current Form 6 guidelines for the documents applicable to their situation.

  • Proof of age as accepted under the current guidelines.
  • Proof of residence/address as accepted under the current guidelines.
  • Recent photograph, where required.

Examples of supporting documents may include Aadhaar Card, passport or other documents accepted under the current ECI guidelines. Requirements can vary, so always check the official Form 6 instructions.

Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2026

SIR stands for Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls.

It is an electoral-roll revision exercise conducted by the Election Commission to help verify and update electoral-roll information.

What should voters know?

  • Check the official ECI voter portal for current SIR information.
  • Verify whether your name appears in the relevant electoral roll.
  • Follow the instructions and forms provided by the Election Commission.
  • Do not rely on unofficial social-media messages for SIR-related information.
⚠️ SIR procedures and applicability can depend on the state/area and the current ECI programme. Always check the official ECI portal for the latest instructions.

📝 How to Apply as a New Voter

Basic steps for a new voter application

1

Visit Official Portal

Open the official Election Commission Voters' Services Portal.

2

Select Form 6

Choose the new voter registration option and complete Form 6.

3

Provide Details

Enter the requested personal, address and other information.

4

Upload Documents

Provide the required supporting documents according to the current Form 6 guidelines.

5

Submit Application

Submit the completed application through the official portal.

6

Track Application

Keep your acknowledgement/reference information and check the application status through official services.

⚠️ Important

VoteSmart is an awareness and educational platform. It does not itself register voters or issue voter ID cards. For actual registration, correction, electoral-roll searches and other official services, always use the Election Commission of India and its official voter-service portals.
