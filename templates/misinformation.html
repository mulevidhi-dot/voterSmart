Common Myths vs. Official Facts

❌ Myth: You can vote online or via SMS

Fact: Voting in India is conducted in person at designated polling stations using EVMs with VVPAT verification.

✅ Fact: A Voter ID card alone isn't enough

Fact: Your name must be registered in the official Electoral Roll. Always verify your name on voterportal.eci.gov.in before polling day.

❌ Myth: Non-citizens can vote with Aadhaar

Fact: Aadhaar is proof of identity, not citizenship. Only registered Indian citizens aged 18 and above can vote.

✅ Fact: Alternate IDs are allowed

Fact: If your name is in the roll, you can vote using alternate approved photo IDs like a PAN card, Driving License, or Passport.

Always Rely on Official Election Commission Sources

When in doubt, consult official portals for voter verification and candidate details.
